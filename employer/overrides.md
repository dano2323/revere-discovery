## Index
`Index = 0.25 × Rank + 0.25 × Sentiment + 0.25 × EVP Pillars + 0.25 × Choice Criteria`, each 0–100 (Category Briefs; not to be relitigated).

## Rank
- **Ranked lists are 10 items** in a Discovery.
- **Scoring (proposed, `docs/01-audit.md` Q4):** 10 points for first, down to 1 for tenth, 0 if not named; the mean across all ranked answers × 10 → 0–100. Computed from the list text, not the platform's average position (which imputes 11 for absent).
- **Appearance rate travels with Rank:** the share of ranked answers that name the employer at all. It is not an Index input.

## Default set
Employer template: 8 sentiment questions (several name the competitors); 12 EVP Pillars; ranked lists Best and Most Recommended; Choice Criteria generated by the model. The requester may add or remove items.

## Where this measure sits (slide 3)
Three lanes: **engagement surveys** (what employees report inside), **review platforms** (Glassdoor, Indeed), **the model layer** (what AI says when a candidate asks). Then Discovery · Full audit · Monitoring.

## Reader and framing
- CHROs, heads of talent, employer-brand leads, and the agencies and service providers that serve them.
- **The CHRO question:** "Are we losing the people we want, to whom, and why?" Lead with how the employer is described against its talent rivals, not with how often it is named.
- Readers check against Glassdoor; expect them to.
- Hold Pillars (the workplace once inside) and Choice Criteria (the decision to join) apart.
