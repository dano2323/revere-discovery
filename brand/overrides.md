## Index
`Index = 0.35 × Visibility + 0.35 × Sentiment + 0.15 × Brand Pillars + 0.15 × Purchase Criteria`, each 0–100. Follows the Category Briefs; if they change the brand weights, this changes with them. Visibility and Sentiment carry equal weight because being named and being recommended are separate outcomes: that is the brand line's lead framing.

## Product mode
- **Product Dimensions replace Brand Pillars,** including in the Index: they take the 15% weight (Mike, 5 Nov).
- **Purchase Criteria still run** in Product mode (Mike, 5 Nov).
- Five dimensions: Revere sets Reputation, Value and Product Characteristics; the model adds two; the model writes each dimension's subdimensions. Scored per subdimension, rolled up per dimension.
- The Pillars slide shows the five dimensions as rows; subdimensions go to the evidence panel.
- `build_extract.py` reads Product Dimensions once a Product-mode export is in hand (the section's keys are not yet known).

## Default set
Standard Template: 12 sentiment questions; the standard Brand Pillars; Visibility attributes Top, Best and Most Recommended; Purchase Criteria generated by the model (leave the template's list empty). The requester may add or remove items.

## Where this measure sits (slide 3)
Three lanes: **brand trackers and surveys** (what customers say when asked), **reviews and social** (what people post), **the model layer** (what AI says when a buyer asks). Then Discovery · Full audit · Monitoring.

## Reader
CMO, brand and comms leads, and their agencies.
